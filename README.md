# Lagos Spots

A real directory of places around Lagos — restaurants, hangouts, study spots, and
events — that people can search, filter, and add to.

## Description

Lagos Spots helps people discover and share places worth visiting around Lagos.
Instead of relying on scattered recommendations across social media and group
chats, users can browse a searchable, filterable directory of spots, see what
category they fall under, and contribute new places they've found themselves.

## Target Audience

- Lagos residents looking for new places to eat, study, or hang out
- Newcomers to Lagos who don't yet have a network to ask for recommendations
- Students searching for reliable study spots with good wifi and seating
- Event-goers wanting a single place to discover what's happening around the city

## Categories

- **Food** — restaurants, cafes, street food spots, and hidden gems
- **Study** — quiet cafes, libraries, and co-working spaces with good wifi
- **Nightlife** — bars, lounges, and clubs for evenings out
- **Events** — pop-ups, concerts, markets, and one-off happenings around the city
